Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what personal information SmartRecruit collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Who We Are

SmartRecruit is a recruitment and talent matchmaking platform that connects job seekers with employers. We operate the website at web-production-d57d5.up.railway.app and any associated subdomains (the "Platform").

For questions about this policy, contact us at: privacy@smartrecruit.app

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information you provide directly

  • • Account details: email address, hashed password, account role (candidate or employer)
  • • Candidate profile: full name, age, birthday, phone number, location, biography, profession, skills, education, certifications, languages, salary expectations, LinkedIn and portfolio URLs
  • • CV / resume: uploaded as a file stored securely in our database
  • • Profile picture: uploaded image stored in our database
  • • Employer profile: company name, industry, size, description, work environment, benefits, contact details, company logo
  • • Job posts: title, description, required skills, salary range, location, job type
  • • Messages: content of outreach messages and replies exchanged between employers and candidates
  • • Applications: cover letters submitted when applying for positions
  • • Ratings and reviews: scores and written reviews submitted about employers and candidates

Information collected automatically

  • • Session tokens: a secure cookie is set when you log in, used solely to keep you signed in
  • • Standard server logs: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps — retained for security and debugging purposes only

Information we do NOT collect

  • ✗ We do not store plaintext passwords — passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage
  • ✗ We do not collect payment or financial information
  • ✗ We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies
  • ✗ We do not sell your data to third parties

3. How We Use Your Information

  • • To operate the platform: creating your account, matching you with relevant jobs or candidates, and enabling communication between employers and candidates
  • • Profile matching: your profile text (title, skills, resume) is embedded into vectors used solely for relevance ranking in candidate search — this data is stored in our Qdrant vector database and is not shared externally
  • • Notifications: to send you in-platform alerts about messages, application status changes, and job matches
  • • Security: to detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access
  • • Service improvement: anonymous usage patterns may be used to improve the platform

4. Who Can See Your Information

Candidates: Your public profile (name, title, skills, bio, education, certifications, location) is visible to logged-in employers. Your email address, age, birthday, phone number, and CV are not shown publicly — employers can only download your CV if you have uploaded one and they view your profile.

Employers: Your company name, industry, description, work environment, benefits, and active job posts are visible to all logged-in users. Your contact name and email are not displayed publicly.

Messages: Messages between employers and candidates are only visible to the two parties involved in that conversation.

Ratings: Ratings and reviews are publicly visible on profiles but the identity of the reviewer is not disclosed.

Staff: Authorised platform administrators may access account information for the purpose of customer support, abuse prevention, and legal compliance.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon (a cloud database provider) and a vector database hosted on Qdrant Cloud. The platform itself is hosted on Railway. All connections use TLS/SSL encryption in transit.

We implement the following security measures:

  • • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plaintext)
  • • Session tokens are cryptographically random and expire after 30 days
  • • All user inputs are sanitized to prevent SQL injection and XSS attacks
  • • File uploads (CVs and avatars) are stored as binary data in the database, not as publicly accessible files
  • • HTTPS enforced on all connections

While we take security seriously, no system is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • • Your profile, messages, applications, and uploaded files are permanently deleted from our database
  • • Your search vector is removed from Qdrant
  • • Server logs containing your IP address are deleted within 30 days
  • • Ratings you have given others will be anonymised rather than deleted

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access You can view and download all your profile information at any time from your profile page.
Correction You can edit your profile information at any time from the Edit Profile page.
Visibility Candidates can toggle searchability and open-to-work status at any time to control who can find their profile.
Deletion You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at privacy@smartrecruit.app. We will process the request within 30 days.
Portability You may request a copy of your personal data in a machine-readable format by contacting us.

8. Cookies

We use a single session cookie named sr_session to keep you logged in. This cookie:

  • • Is set only when you sign in
  • • Expires after 30 days or when you sign out
  • • Is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax for security
  • • Contains only a random token — no personal data is stored in the cookie itself

We also store your theme preference (dark/light) and language choice in localStorage — this stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics.

9. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party infrastructure providers to operate the platform:

Provider Purpose Data shared
Railway App hosting Server logs, environment variables
Neon PostgreSQL database All user data in the database
Qdrant Cloud Vector search Profile text embeddings (anonymised)
Tailwind CSS CDN Styling Your IP address (standard CDN request)

All providers are contractually bound to process data only as instructed and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

10. Children's Privacy

SmartRecruit is not intended for use by persons under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has registered an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and notify registered users via an in-platform notification. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns:

privacy@smartrecruit.app

We aim to respond to all privacy requests within 30 days.
